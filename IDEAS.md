# Ideas for later versions

Not promises, just things worth trying. Open an issue if you want one of them.

## Events

- **Sunrise and sunset moments:** for a few minutes around sunrise or sunset, the corner shows an
  animated sun rising or setting instead of the static icon.
- **Weather on the whole panel:** while it rains, snows or storms, lay the firmware's built-in
  overlay (`rain`, `snow`, `storm`, `drizzle`, `thunder`, `frost`) over the clock, optionally only
  for a moment each minute.
- **Readings that call for attention:** a reading turns a colour, blinks or briefly takes the panel
  when it matches a value (`Open`) or crosses a threshold (`> 1500 ppm`).

## Corners

- **More forecast options:** rain chance tomorrow, high/low tomorrow, wind direction, UV index.
- **Colour by value:** temperatures from blue to red, readings by thresholds set per reading.
- **Bigger panels:** a larger time font when the panel is taller than 16 rows.
- **Buttons:** left and right flip a corner between alternatives, e.g. weather now and tomorrow.

## Icons

- **Fog and thunderstorm:** the fog lines read as an equals sign and the bolt is small; both could
  be clearer.
- **Partly cloudy and cloudy:** clearer cloud shapes at 8×8.
- **Partly cloudy at night:** a crescent behind the cloud instead of a round moon.
- **Clear night:** the crescent alone can read as a bracket; a few stars beside it would help.

## Project

- **Offline preview:** render frames on a computer with a stubbed panel API, for screenshots and
  pixel tests without a clock.
- **Hub listing:** keep the AWTRIX Hub page in step with each release.
